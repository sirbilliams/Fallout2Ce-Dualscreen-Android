Fallout 2 Community Edition for Android, built for the AYN Thor and other dual-screen handhelds: the game on the top screen, a touch companion on the bottom. Bring your own copy of Fallout 2; no game files are included.

**Install:** download `Fallout2-Dualscreen.apk` below. It installs over 2.0 and keeps your saves and settings.

## Fixes
- **Killed enemies staying alive after a second attack.** In combat, tapping a second attack on the touch map or bottom combat panel while the first attack's animation was still running re-initialised the attack record and wiped the first attack's damage. The target played its death animation but stayed alive: in the Temple of Trials, a killed Giant Ant came back unhurt and combat restarted. Attacks, End Turn and End Combat are now ignored until the attack animation is over, as in the original game.

Regression test: dev autotest scenario `doubleattack`.

No other changes.
