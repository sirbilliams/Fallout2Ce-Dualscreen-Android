Fallout 2 Community Edition for Android, built for the AYN Thor and other dual-screen handhelds: the game on the top screen, a touch companion on the bottom. Bring your own copy of Fallout 2; no game files are included.

**Install:** download `Fallout2-Dualscreen.apk` below. It installs over earlier builds of this port and keeps your saves and settings.

## Highlights
- **Pip-Boy 2000 theme**, now the default on first launch: a green CRT look drawn from the game's own art, with a reduce-motion option. Classic is one tap away in Settings > Display.
- **Mouse controls**: the cursor follows your finger on the top screen, a bottom-screen TOUCHPAD page works like a laptop trackpad, and the right stick drives a cursor (A / R3 click, B / R3 hold right click).
- **Controller remapping**: Settings > Controls has a picker for every button, plus Reset controls.
- **Polish pass over every bottom screen**: settings, dashboard, inventory, character, barter, loot, dialogue and Pip-Boy screens all fit their text and controls in both themes.
- **Controller clicks on the map fixed**: A and R3 now click in the world on a real Thor. Patch, analysis and tests by **Ocherk**. Thank you!

## Also new
- Live map on the bottom screen with a legend, elevation browsing and a town area list.
- Dialogue and barter can sit on the bottom screen, with a quantity picker.
- First-run setup guide on Android: game files, layout and theme.
- Quality-of-life options: tap to skip speech, end dialogue on goodbye.
- "Game saved" style messages now show on the top screen, so they no longer cover bottom-screen buttons.

## Fixes
- Saves list in the right order.
- Overlapping and clipped elements on the dashboard and in settings.
